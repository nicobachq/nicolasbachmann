§ About

Between Lugano and Buenos Aires.

I am a Swiss–Uruguayan filmmaker who has lived most of his adult life between Argentina and Switzerland.

I have a strong background in directing, production coordination and media management, with experience working with clients world-wide. In 2014 I returned to Switzerland where I collaborated with Ledfilms, a Lugano-based production company. Since then I have also worked independently as a filmmaker and photographer for clients in Switzerland and abroad.

I started my working experience at the Mar del Plata International Film Festival, a category-A festival, as a production coordinator — supervising special guests, official jury activities and day-to-day planning of the festival's events including master classes, while working closely with press agencies to expand the festival's reach.

During film school I co-founded an audio-visual production company in Buenos Aires, coordinating teams on projects for La Nación, Unilever Latin America, Planeta DeAgostini and several advertising agencies. We also undertook a five-year project together with an art gallery and UNICEF, involving elite sportsmen to raise funds for the underprivileged.

I served as key production assistant on Police Certifiable, the concert film of The Police's 2007–08 reunion tour, and on Better Than Therapy, a documentary about the same reunion. I directed the making-of for Felicitas, an Argentine feature, and for Panamerican Dreams, selected at the Beverly Hills Film Festival. I was co-producer and co-director of A Patagonia Tale, a documentary short selected at the Cannes Short Corner, Four Docs, the DLA Film Festival and Rushes Soho Short Festival.

I enjoy working closely with others — as part of a team or as a leader. Fifteen years across different fields gives me a unique approach to a client's goal. As a filmmaker, I look for projects that explore the limits of humanity's search for spiritual truth in ways that reveal new perspectives on social, political and environmental events.
